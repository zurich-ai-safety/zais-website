What it is

The AI Safety Fundamentals programme is designed to make the space of AI safety and governance risks from advanced AI more accessible. Since the field is so new, there are no textbooks or widespread university courses — we're looking to fill that gap.

We bring together experts and knowledgeable facilitators with participants to discuss 6 weeks of curated reading that together introduce the field, through introduction lectures and active weekly discussions in small groups of 4–5 people with an experienced facilitator.

Our curriculum is largely adapted from the original programme by BlueDot Impact.

Who it is for

Anyone with an interest in ensuring AI development happens safely — whether from a technical or policy perspective — is welcome to apply.

A basic knowledge of ML is useful but not required; several past participants completed the programme with no prior background. Applicants can indicate their level of knowledge so we can match them with similarly-experienced peers.

Curriculum

Seven weekly topics, from first principles to governance.

00
AI and the Years Ahead
How and why might we build future AI systems? Covers neural networks, gradient descent, transformers, and how these techniques train today's LLMs.
01
What Is AI Alignment?
What do we need to ensure AI systems do what we want, and why is this difficult? Covers outer/inner alignment, agents, and convergent instrumental goals.
02
Reinforcement Learning from Human (or AI) Feedback
Why do today's AI systems mostly do what we want? A dive into RLHF.
03
Scalable Oversight
How might we scale human feedback for more powerful, complex models? Covers iterated amplification, debate, and weak-to-strong generalization.
04
Robustness, Unlearning, and Control
Further techniques to make AI systems safe — adversarial robustness, unlearning harmful knowledge, and controlling risk from potentially deceptive models.
05
Mechanistic Interpretability
How might we understand what's going on inside an AI model? Covers circuit analysis and dictionary learning.
06
Technical Governance Approaches
How might we measure and mitigate deployment risks? Covers pre-deployment testing, AI control techniques, and global coordination.

Curriculum baseline: course.aisafetyfundamentals.com/alignment. Groups and facilitators may skip or supplement readings based on skill level and interests.
