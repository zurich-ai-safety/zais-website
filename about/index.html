About ZAIS

We are a community of students, researchers, and professionals working on the long-term safety of artificial intelligence. Our goal is to help ensure that future, more powerful AI systems are developed safely, for everyone.

ZAIS is run by a small, rotating team of volunteers — students and professionals based in Zurich who organise programmes, events, and community activities alongside their studies or day jobs.

2022 — 2024

The grassroots roots.

Community emergence

The group originates at ETH Zurich, out of overlaps between Effective Altruism Switzerland and alternative technology student groups. A core community of students, machine learning researchers, and physics graduates forms to discuss long-term AGI risk and alignment.

Early activity is entirely educational: informal reading groups, introductory AI safety curricula, and technical seminars on campus.

July 2025

From reading group to organisation.

Strategic formalisation

The organisation starts formalising its event-hosting infrastructure. Core co-organisers build relationships with international safety organisations such as BlueDot Impact to widen career pipelines out of Zurich.

Funding from Open Philanthropy secures the first large-scale, public-facing conference.

27 September 2025

Zurich AI Safety Day 2025.

The public debut

A single day at the ETH Zurich Hauptgebäude becomes the organisation's public debut, drawing over 200 European researchers and developers.

An Org Fair brings together 20+ institutions from across the sector, including the UK AI Safety Institute, Apollo Research, FAR.AI, and Palisade Research.

A partnership with Anthropic hands compute credits to local research teams working on human–AI persuasion and jailbreak audits.

March 2026

Building a bridge to industry.

Towards structural leadership

To close the gap between corporate tech and research, Zurich AI Safety opens global applications for a full-time Director.

The operational focus shifts towards 1-on-1 career advising for senior software and ML engineers at Zurich tech hubs.

July 2026

A Director takes the helm.

New leadership

Tech ecosystem builder Patrick Widmann steps into the formal Director role, guiding the organisation into its multi-day event era.

Programmes, partnerships, and the career pipeline move onto a single, coordinated footing.

7–8 November 2026

Swiss AI Safety Days 2026.

Broadening the scope

The local day scales into a multi-day national initiative at ETH Zurich, built for researchers and professionals rather than a public science fair.

Attendance moves to an application-only format, capped at 300+ participants.

Why Zurich

Zurich isn't an obvious AI hub.
It's arguably the AI hub.

The city is home to ETH Zurich, one of the world's leading science and technology universities, and hosts offices for several of the world's leading AI companies.

Add Switzerland's long tradition of neutrality and diplomacy, and Zurich becomes something rare: a genuinely global meeting point for AI safety, where researchers, companies, and policymakers can meet on neutral ground.

That density is exactly why ZAIS exists here.

Peaks

Where we work: PEAKS

Much of ZAIS's community life happens at PEAKS, a coworking space in the heart of Zurich for people working in AI safety and effective altruism. The space offers meeting rooms and a library of AI safety and EA reading — plus a steady calendar of talks, lightning events, and casual coworking sessions run together with groups like EA Zurich and Apollo Research.
