What is AI Safety?

AI Safety is about making sure AI systems do what we intend and don't cause serious harm, especially as they become more capable and act more independently.

Why now

With advanced AI, we may not get a second chance to fix mistakes.

Related fields and pillars

AI Safety is distinct from AI Ethics and Security, and rests on two pillars.

AI Safety focuses on risks from autonomous AI systems, rather than (mis)use of AI as a tool. AI Safety work splits into technical safety and governance.

  • AI Ethics

    Is AI transparent and unbiased?

    Bias audits, privacy, explainability, accountability

  • AI Safety

    How do we prevent severe, large-scale harm from more capable and autonomous AI?

  • AI Security

    How do we protect AI systems, and prevent misuse?

    Securing model weights and infrastructure, red-teaming, defending against prompt injection
