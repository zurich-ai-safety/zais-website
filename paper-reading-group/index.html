What it is

ZAIS meets regularly to discuss topics in AI alignment and AI safety, with a general focus on the technical aspects and implications of recent research. We also organise workshop meetings where people discuss project ideas and anything else they're interested in.

When & where

Check our calendar and join the WhatsApp community to be notified about upcoming sessions.

Who it is for

Anyone with an interest in ensuring AI development happens safely, whether from a technical or policy perspective, is welcome to join.

Because discussions rely on familiarity with key terms in the field, we recommend some experience in ML and AI safety for the best experience. If you've completed our AI Safety Fundamentals programme (or an equivalent), you're well-suited to join — check the Fundamentals curriculum if you're unsure you meet the bar.
