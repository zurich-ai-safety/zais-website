Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Zurich AI Safety (ZAIS) is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data.

1. Who We Are

Controller: Zurich AI Safety, a Swiss non-profit association (Verein)

Contact:
Zeughausstrasse 31
8004 Zurich, Switzerland
Email: info@aisafety.ch

Fiscal Sponsor: Zurich AI Safety operates under fiscal sponsorship from Effective Altruism Switzerland. For matters related to grants and compliance, EA Switzerland may assist, but ZAIS is the primary data controller for website and event data.

2. What Data We Collect

Website Visitors

  • IP address, browser type, pages visited, time spent on page
  • Referral source (how you arrived at our site)
  • General location (country/city level, not precise)

How: Google Analytics 4 (see section 6 below)

Event Attendees

  • Name, email, institutional affiliation (for registration)
  • Professional background and research interests (if provided in application)
  • Dietary requirements and accessibility needs (if disclosed)
  • Photos during events (if you attend)

Community Members

  • If you join our WhatsApp community or mailing list, we store your contact details and communication preferences
  • Slack: We do not store community member data from external Slack workspaces connected to ZAIS. Slack is independent of this website and governed by Slack's privacy policy.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To organise and run events and programmes
  • To send you information about upcoming activities (only if you've opted in)
  • To understand how our website is used and improve it
  • To comply with legal and tax obligations (via EA Switzerland for fiscal sponsorship matters)

4. Legal Basis

Under Swiss privacy law (revFADP), we process your data based on:

  • Consent: Event registration, newsletter sign-ups, photography
  • Contract: Administering events and programmes you've registered for
  • Legal obligation: Tax and compliance records (held by EA Switzerland)
  • Legitimate interest: Website analytics to improve our services

5. Photography at Events

We may publish photos from ZAIS events and activities for promotional and community purposes. If you appear in a photo and prefer not to have it published, please contact us at info@aisafety.ch and we'll remove it promptly.

6. Google Analytics 4

ZAIS uses Google Analytics 4 to measure how visitors use zurich.aisafety.ch. This helps us understand which pages are most visited, how long people spend reading content, and which events generate the most interest.

Data Collected:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each
  • Geographic location (country/city level)
  • Device type and browser
  • Traffic source (direct, search, referral)

Data Retention:

14 months (Google's default)

Your Rights:

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing Google's browser extension. Google's own privacy policy covers how they handle the data.

Our Settings:

ZAIS has not enabled features that share data back to Google for advertising or product improvement. We use analytics only to understand website usage.

7. Third-Party Services

Service Purpose Data Shared
Google Analytics Website traffic analysis Pages, location, device, referral source
Luma (events calendar) Event registration and ticketing Name, email, event interest (if you register)
WhatsApp Community communication Phone number, name (governed by WhatsApp's privacy policy)
GitHub Pages Website hosting No personal data stored by GitHub for this site

8. Data Retention

  • Event registration: Kept for 2 years after event, then deleted
  • Website analytics: 14 months (Google's retention policy)
  • Mailing list/WhatsApp: Kept as long as you remain subscribed
  • Tax/compliance records: 7 years (Swiss legal requirement)

9. Your Rights

Under Swiss law, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request what personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Ask us to update or correct inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from newsletters or event communications at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at info@aisafety.ch.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, alteration, or disclosure. Our website uses standard encryption (HTTPS) for data in transit. Event data is stored securely and access is limited to core ZAIS organisers.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "last updated" date above. Your continued use of our website constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Questions?

If you have any questions about how we handle your data, please contact us at info@aisafety.ch.
