This page adds what is specific to this website, zurich.aisafety.ch.

1. Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, owned by Microsoft, USA). When you visit it, GitHub logs your IP address and browser details for security purposes. Data is transferred to the USA on the basis of standard contractual clauses (GitHub Data Protection Agreement).

2. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how this website is used: which pages are visited, for how long, from which kind of device, from which country or city, and how visitors arrived (direct, search or a link). Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (_ga and _ga_*) to tell visits apart. The data is kept for 14 months. We have not enabled advertising features or data sharing with Google for other purposes.

Google processes the data in the USA and worldwide, under the Swiss-US Data Privacy Framework. We rely on our legitimate interest in improving the website. You can object at any time by blocking or deleting cookies in your browser, or by installing Google's opt-out browser add-on.

3. Contact and your rights

For questions about ZAIS, write to zurich@aisafety.ch. To exercise your data protection rights, you can also write to EA Geneva at hello@effectivealtruism.ch. If you believe your rights are not respected, you can contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).
