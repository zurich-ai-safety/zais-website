OUR MISSION

Zurich AI Safety exists to bring more talent into AI safety by attracting people to the field, upskilling them through rigorous programmes, and connecting them to real opportunities in Zurich. We build the infrastructure a growing field needs: talks, courses, reading groups, a flagship conference, and a shared home base, so that curiosity about AI safety turns into sustained, local contribution.

WHY ZURICH

Zurich is home to ETH Zurich and a dense cluster of AI research labs, startups, and policy institutions, making it one of the best-connected places in Europe to work on AI safety.

Being embedded in this ecosystem means faster feedback loops, easier collaboration, and a shorter path from idea to impact.
