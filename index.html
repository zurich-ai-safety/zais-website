Where To Start

Take your first step to a future in AI safety.

Ready to help shape a secure, beneficial future with advanced AI? Join one of our programs or be part of our events. We equip you with the technical skills and policy frameworks needed to ensure frontier AI remains safe.

WHY ZURICH

Zurich is home to ETH Zurich and its leading AI research, frontier AI labs and innovation, making it one of the best-connected places in Europe to work on AI safety.

A technical hub with local world-class talent closely connected to the policy and governance work happening in Bern and Geneva.
