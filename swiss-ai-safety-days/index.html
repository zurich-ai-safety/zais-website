Flagship Event

Swiss AI Safety Days 2026

The next step in your AI safety career is here.

Two days of talks, workshops, an organisation fair, and high-density networking across AI safety research, governance, and careers. Whether it's your first or your fifth, Europe's AI safety community meets in Zurich — and ZAIS is one of the organisations behind it.

Official Road to Geneva Event

Part of the lead-up to the Geneva AI Summit 2027.

Swiss AI Safety Days is an official Road to Geneva event — one of the gatherings building toward the Geneva AI Summit in June 2027. Switzerland's tradition of diplomacy makes it a natural intermediary among the world's leading AI powers, and that's exactly the ground this conference stands on.

2026

The event doubles to two full days.

The full programme lands later this year. Three things are already certain:

01

Talks & Workshops

Learn from the people shaping the field. Talks and hands-on workshops across technical AI safety, governance, entrepreneurship, and careers.

02

Organisation Fair

Meet the organisations working on AI safety, face to face. The 2025 edition led directly to new roles and collaborations across the ecosystem.

03

High-Density Networking

Skip the small talk. Structured 1-1s, speed networking, and roundtables built to get you the connections that matter.

Confirmed for 2026

Who you'll hear from.

The speaker list is updated every week as more names are confirmed.

speaker portrait — Stuart Russell
KEYNOTE
Stuart Russell
Distinguished Professor of Computer Science, UC Berkeley · Director, Center for Human-Compatible AI
Co-author of Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach, the standard AI textbook used in over 1,500 universities across 135 countries, and a Fellow of the Royal Society. One of the field's leading voices on keeping advanced AI under human control.
speaker portrait — Nora Ammann
SPEAKER
Nora Ammann
Programme Director, ARIA · Co-founder, Principles of Intelligence
Runs Safeguarded AI, a roughly £60M ARIA programme building a mathematical assurance toolkit for formally verified AI outputs. Co-author of Gradual Disempowerment; previously Research Manager at Oxford's FHI.
speaker portrait — Adrian Hutter
SPEAKER
Adrian Hutter
Lead, Gemini Safety Post-Training, Google DeepMind
Leads the Gemini safety post-training team, with research centred on instilling robust values in AI models across diverse environments. Background in theoretical quantum physics.
speaker portrait — Susan Nesbitt
SPEAKER
Susan Nesbitt
Managing Director, Cantellus Group
Advises governments and technology companies on the governance and responsible deployment of agentic AI. Serves on IASEAI's Policy Committee and OpenAI's External Red Teaming Network; previously WhatsApp and the World Economic Forum.
speaker portrait — Christian Schroeder de Witt
SPEAKER
Christian Schroeder de Witt
Principal Investigator, Oxford Witt Lab, University of Oxford
Defined the field of multi-agent security. Earlier work on perfectly secure steganography was named a 2023 Biggest Discovery in Computer Science by Quanta Magazine.
speaker portrait — Robert West
SPEAKER
Robert West
Associate Professor, EPFL · Head of the Data Science and AI Lab
Works at the intersection of AI, NLP, and computational social science, with a focus on building AI that is safe for humanity.

Venue

The venue: ETH Zurich.

We return to the main building of ETH Zurich, one of the world's leading universities for science and technology. 21 Nobel laureates walked these halls, Albert Einstein among them. In November 2026, it's your turn.

The main building sits in the centre of Zurich, a few minutes from the Polybahn and well connected by tram.

Application Process

Places are limited, so attendance is by application.

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis with decisions sent throughout, so applying early gives you the best chance of a place. If you're accepted, you'll be invited to pay for your ticket to secure your spot.
